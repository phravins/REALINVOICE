    Sync

    This till bills on its own and sends copies to the back office in the background. Billing never waits for it, so nothing here can stop you raising an invoice.

    Back office address

    Where copies are sent. Leave it empty until you have one — the till keeps every invoice safely queued either way, and sends the backlog once an address is set.

    This till's token

    Register this till in the back office and paste the token it gives you. It is stored on this computer only, and is never shown again once saved — if you lose it, issue a new one.

    Discount Limit

    How much of a bill a cashier may discount on their own, counting line and invoice discounts together. Anything above this needs an owner's username and password before the invoice will save. Owners are never asked.

    Price Lists

    A customer is billed from the list they are assigned to, or from the default if they have none. An item with no rate on a list is billed at its base rate, so a list only has to price what it actually changes.
